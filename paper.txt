                                  Abstract

 001     Cross-domain few-shot object detection (CD-FSOD) adapts
 002     a pretrained detector to unseen domains using only a
 003     few labeled examples. Existing methods transfer visual
 004     prototypes, generated data, or multimodal prompts, but
 005     they often treat every support factor as valid adaptation
 006     evidence. We identify support-induced context leakage:
 007     changing only the support background can alter query
 008     classification and localization, even when the query im-
 009     age and support foreground remain fixed. We propose
 010     a knowledge-enhanced Hard-Soft framework that regu-
 011     lates both the evidence being transferred and its authority
 012     over model decisions. Foreground-Frozen Context Probing
 013     (FFCP) estimates class-wise context-sensitive subspaces
 014     through label-preserving support interventions. Context-
 015     Constrained Hard-Soft Decomposition (CHSD) constructs
 016     a Hard identity anchor and a reliability-weighted Soft
 017     target-domain prototype after removing context-sensitive
 018     directions. Asymmetric Task-Authority Routing (ATAR)
